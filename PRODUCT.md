# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** recruiters and hiring managers at German/EU companies hiring AI engineers. They skim fast and decide in under a minute whether Harsh is worth a call.
- **Secondary:** engineers and the AI community arriving from a shared link or GitHub, judging taste and depth.

## Product Purpose

Sell Harsh Virani. The site is a pitch, not a CV: it should make a visitor think "this person is exceptional, I want to talk to him" and make reaching out effortless. It curates; it does not catalogue.

## Positioning

An AI engineer who builds agents that actually work in the real world, and ships them end to end, from model to product. Thinks like a researcher (his thesis studies deep-research LLM agents), builds like a product engineer (live products with real users). The angle a generic portfolio can't copy: a builder of reliable AI agents with the taste to make them feel effortless.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, job applications, CV links, and GitHub.
- Bilingual: English and German, with a toggle.
- Content lives in a headless CMS and updates without a redeploy (Next.js on-demand revalidation via webhook).
- A handful of hero projects told as stories, not a grid of everything.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind v4, Vercel. Fresh rebuild; reuse content only.
- Headless CMS (vendor open; Sanity is the leading candidate).
- One AI-native signature interaction on an otherwise minimal page (concept open).
- Light and dark themes.

## Brand Commitments

- Personality: curious tinkerer, calm and precise, bold and ambitious.
- Beautiful, minimal, premium, unmistakably his. Never a generic developer template (the old React Bits template look is the anti-reference).
- Voice: confident and inviting. Sells the work.

## Material to Draw From

A pool to pick the strongest pieces from, not a checklist to display in full:

- Thesis: deep-research LLM agents for office work (spreadsheet agents benchmarked against SWE-agent, Claude Code, Cline).
- Agents and AI: on-prem RAG for privacy-bound clients, agentic support agent, Zero-Touch customs filing (LangGraph), agent reliability testing platform, AI lead-qualification workflow.
- Shipped products: SSRI assessment platform (live, paying users), PocketPulse PWA (pocketpulse.me), GoShimmy e-commerce.
- Also: M.Sc. AI & Robotics at Hof (Dean's List), TechStaunch, robotics work, Oracle Agentic AI and Anthropic MCP certifications, hackathon 2nd place.
- Profile photo available.

## Product Principles

1. Sell, don't list: lead with the three or four strongest stories.
2. Remembered in one viewport: one signature idea, everything else quiet.
3. Show the work working: demos and motion over adjectives.
4. Content outlives code: everything a visitor reads is editable in the CMS.
